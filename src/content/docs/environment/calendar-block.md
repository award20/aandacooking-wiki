The **Calendar** (`aandacooking:calendar`) is a placeable wall decoration connected to the [Game Calendar and seasons](/environment/calendar-seasons/).

## Placement

Place the Calendar on the side of a solid block. It faces outward from its supporting wall and cannot be placed on a floor or ceiling. If that wall support is removed, the Calendar can no longer remain attached.

The Calendar is rendered as an in world block with a dedicated renderer. Its display follows the game's calendar information rather than requiring players to run commands to inspect the date.

## Crafting

Use **3 Sticks** across the top row and **3 Paper** across the next row to craft one Calendar.

```text
SSS
PPP
```

See [Equipment & Crafting](/reference/equipment-crafting/) and [Calendar & Seasons](/environment/calendar-seasons/).
