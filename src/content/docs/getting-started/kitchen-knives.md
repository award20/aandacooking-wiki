Kitchen Knives are used at the [Cutting Board](/stations/cutting-board/) to prepare ingredients. Six knife materials are supported.

## Material tiers

| Knife | Durability | Item ID |
|---|---:|---|
| Copper | 150 | `aandacooking:copper_kitchen_knife` |
| Iron | 250 | `aandacooking:iron_kitchen_knife` |
| Gold | 350 | `aandacooking:gold_kitchen_knife` |
| Stainless Steel | 750 | `aandacooking:stainless_steel_kitchen_knife` |
| Diamond Coated | 1250 | `aandacooking:diamond_coated_kitchen_knife` |
| Netherite | 2000 | `aandacooking:netherite_kitchen_knife` |

## Getting the knives

Copper, Iron, Gold, and Stainless Steel knives use a Stick and the corresponding metal Ingot in the same shaped layout. The Stainless Steel version also has an upgrade recipe using an Iron Kitchen Knife and Stainless Steel Ingot.

Upgrade a Stainless Steel Kitchen Knife into a Diamond Coated Knife with a Diamond using a shapeless recipe. A Netherite Knife uses the Smithing Table with a Netherite Upgrade Smithing Template, a Diamond Coated Knife, and a Netherite Ingot.

Stainless Steel Ingot production now begins with [Stainless Steel Blend](/reference/equipment-crafting/), made using Iron, Copper, and Coal or Charcoal, then processed in a Blast Furnace.

## Preparation modes

Use **J** by default to toggle between Simple and Interactive preparation. Simple preparation uses the familiar click action on the board. Interactive preparation follows the cutting guides displayed over the ingredient. See [Cutting Board](/stations/cutting-board/) for details.
