Villages are an important place to discover food, equipment, recipes, and the wider cooking economy in A & A Cooking.

## What you can find

- [Plains Village Market](/village/plains-market/): an additional village style built around a market, plaza, stalls, kitchens, bakery, farmland, and gathering places
- [Chefs & Bakers](/village/chefs-bakers/): villagers with cooking and baking workstations and relevant trade offers
- [Spice Traders](/village/spice-traders/): traveling merchants who visit registered markets and trade goods from other regions
- [Village Bands](/village/bands/): villager musicians who perform music at a market
- [Crates & Cargo](/storage/crates-cargo/): storage and generated supplies used in markets, kitchens, and merchant shipments

Village and market features interact with [Recipe Notes](/recipe-book/notes-discovery/), the [Recipe Book](/recipe-book/overview/), and [World Generation & Discovery](/development/world-generation-discovery/).

## Availability

The dedicated market structure is configured for Plains Village biomes. Villager trades and market activity depend on nearby workstations, registered markets, and the state of the village.
