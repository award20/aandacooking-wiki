Some [Village Markets](/village/overview/) can form a **Village Band** from villagers in the area.

## Band members

A band uses **3-5 members**. Its membership and known songs are tracked with the market, allowing the group to preserve its identity between sessions.

## Music and performances

Bands begin with a small repertoire of **3-5 songs** drawn from supported Minecraft music discs. They choose songs for performances, consider recent history, and can favor certain tracks. Performances include short breaks between songs and interaction with nearby listeners.

## Teaching a song

Use a supported music disc on a band member to teach the band that song. A previously known song is handled differently from a new addition. A newly taught song may become a temporary favorite for **3-7 in game days**, while the band's original favorite is kept for later.

## Requests and controls

An audience can interact with an active performance, and the market commands offer information and test controls for forming bands, dissolving them, skipping a song, and advancing to another song.

See [Commands](/reference/commands/) for the administration commands and [Plains Village Market](/village/plains-market/) for the band stage.
