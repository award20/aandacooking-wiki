The **Plains Village Market** is a village structure focused on cooking, commerce, farming, and community activity. Its world generation uses a collection of connected structure pools rather than a single fixed building.

## Market facilities

Possible market pieces include:

- A plaza and fountain
- Produce stalls, spice stalls, and a pantry stall
- A market storage area
- Small and large Chef kitchens
- Small and large bakeries
- An herb garden and mixed farm
- A merchant wagon and wagon parking area
- A village band performance area
- Connecting roads, paths, lampposts, and other village buildings

Individual layouts can vary because world generation selects from available structures and road pieces. Not every market is expected to contain every variant.

## What to do at a market

Look for the [Chef and Baker villagers](/village/chefs-bakers/), inspect [Crates](/storage/crates-cargo/) for supplies, and return later to see whether a [Spice Trader](/village/spice-traders/) is visiting. Some markets may also host a [Village Band](/village/bands/).

The Plains market is a separate generated structure using the Plains Village biome category. See [World Generation & Discovery](/development/world-generation-discovery/) for other structure loot and discovery systems.
