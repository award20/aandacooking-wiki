A & A Cooking adds two villager professions for its cooking economy: **Chef** and **Baker**.

## Workstations

| Profession | Workstation | Block ID |
|---|---|---|
| Chef | Chef Workstation | `aandacooking:chef_workstation` |
| Baker | Baker Workstation | `aandacooking:baker_workstation` |

Villagers can claim their corresponding workstations. The profession trade lists offer cooking or baking goods, and higher tiers include more elaborate food and opportunities for [Recipe Note](/recipe-book/notes-discovery/) trades.

## Cooking equipment matters

The mod can examine the kitchen equipment near a villager's workstation. Nearby functional stations and bakeware affect which specialized food offers are eligible. Chefs benefit from stations such as the [Stockpot](/cooking/stockpot/), [Skillet](/cooking/skillet/), [Wok](/cooking/wok/), and [Saucepan](/cooking/saucepan/). Bakers benefit from the [Mixing Bowl](/stations/mixing-bowl/), [Stovetop/Oven](/stations/stovetop-oven/), and [Oven Bakeware](/cooking/oven-bakeware/).

When the needed equipment is absent, trade candidates can fall back to a more accessible food. The feature is designed to make a working village kitchen relevant to the trade offers around it.
