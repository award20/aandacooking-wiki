**Spice Traders** are visiting merchants linked to registered [Village Markets](/village/overview/). Their routes, regional origins, visits, trade goods, and shipments are tracked by the world.

## Visits

Markets schedule a first visit after roughly **1-3 in game days**. A trader typically remains for **2-5 days**. Later visits are scheduled with a **3-7 day** wait after departure. These are scheduling ranges, not a guarantee that the trader is standing at the stall at an exact time.

Traders have an arrival, trading, and departure lifecycle. Their region of origin can differ from the destination market, creating opportunities to exchange different regional goods.

## Trade and cargo

A Spice Trader can bring merchandise from an origin region and buy selected goods at the destination. Merchant cargo is associated with [Crates](/storage/crates-cargo/), and completed purchases can be reflected in shipment storage.

The [Spice Box](/storage/crates-cargo/) supplies Salt and assorted seasonings when opened. It is a separate consumable item from the placeable storage Crates.

## Market commands

Market and trader information is available through `/aacooking market` commands. See [Commands](/reference/commands/) for the complete command reference, including administrator testing controls.
