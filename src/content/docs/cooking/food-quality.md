A & A Cooking assigns a quality rating to supported finished foods. The displayed quality depends on how successfully a dish was prepared and cooked.

## Quality tiers

| Tier | Score range | Canonical score |
|---|---:|---:|
| Poor | 0-39 | 25 |
| Normal | 40-69 | 55 |
| Good | 70-89 | 80 |
| Excellent | 90-100 | 100 |

These tiers classify and normalize quality scores. Different recipes and cooking stations may calculate quality using their own timing, temperature, and preparation rules.

For food aging and storage, see [Freshness & Spoilage](/storage/freshness-spoilage/). Food quality and freshness are related to different aspects of the experience.
