---
title: Cookware Tiers
description: Material progression and thermal multipliers for Stockpots, Saucepans, Skillets, and Woks.
---

A & A Cooking includes four material tiers for each heated stovetop cookware family. Higher tiers heat faster and cool more slowly than Iron cookware while keeping the same recipe role and inventory layout for that vessel type.

## Tier multipliers

| Tier | Heating rate | Cooling rate |
|---|---:|---:|
| Iron | 1.00x | 1.00x |
| Stainless Steel | 1.15x | 0.90x |
| Diamond Coated | 1.30x | 0.80x |
| Netherite | 1.45x | 0.70x |

A heating multiplier above `1.00x` means the vessel approaches the active heat source faster. A lower cooling multiplier means it loses stored heat more slowly after the heat source is reduced or removed.

## Available cookware

Each tier is implemented for:

- [Stockpot](/cooking/stockpot/)
- [Saucepan](/cooking/saucepan/)
- [Skillet](/cooking/skillet/)
- [Wok](/cooking/wok/)

The material tier changes thermal response. Recipe matching, ingredient capacity, liquid capacity, toss behavior, and other vessel specific mechanics still come from the cookware type itself.

## Upgrade path

The current crafting progression is:

1. Iron cookware
2. Stainless Steel cookware
3. Diamond Coated cookware
4. Netherite cookware

Stainless Steel versions use one Stainless Steel Ingot with the Iron vessel. Diamond Coated versions use one Diamond with the Stainless Steel vessel. Netherite versions use one Netherite Ingot with the Diamond Coated vessel.

See [Equipment & Crafting](/reference/equipment-crafting/) for the full crafting reference.
