Rice and Soybean form one of A & A Cooking's largest connected ingredient families. They are not single purpose crops: both feed into processing chains, cooked components, fermented ingredients, and complete dishes.

## Rice chain

The Rice foundation includes:

- Rice and Rice Seeds
- [Cooked Rice](/recipes/details/cooked-rice/)
- Rice Flour
- Rice Porridge variants
- Rice Pancake Batter and Rice Pancakes
- Rice Cake Dough and Seared Rice Cake
- Rice bowls
- Rice bread
- multiple fried Rice dishes

Rice therefore acts as a staple ingredient, a flour source, a cooked base, and a prerequisite for more complex meals.

## Soybean chain

The Soybean foundation includes:

- Soybean and Soybean Seeds
- Soybean Puree
- Soy Milk
- Sweet Soy Milk
- [Fresh Tofu](/recipes/details/fresh-tofu/)
- Seared Tofu
- Fermented Soybeans
- Soy Sauce Mash
- Soy Sauce
- Sweet Soy Glaze
- fermented and pickled ingredients used by later dishes

The Soybean chain spans the [Blender](/stations/blender/), direct cooking stations, and the [Fermentation Crock](/stations/fermentation-crock/).
