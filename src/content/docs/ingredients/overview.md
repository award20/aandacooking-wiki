A & A Cooking includes a large ingredient and food catalog spanning raw produce, prepared ingredients, dairy, herbs, Rice, Soybean, sauces, doughs, preserved foods, drinks, and finished meals.

For the exhaustive registered item list, see [Complete Content Index](/reference/content-index/).

## Ingredient preparation

Ingredients can carry recipe relevant state such as:

- washed or unwashed
- whole
- sliced
- diced
- minced
- julienned
- shredded
- cooking state and doneness
- ingredient quality

[Ingredient Preparation](/getting-started/ingredient-preparation/) explains the preparation system and [Cutting Board](/stations/cutting-board/) interactions.

## Ingredient first IDs

Prepared items generally group under their parent ingredient. Examples include:

- `tomato_sauce`
- `tomato_paste`
- `onion_powder`
- `milk_cheese_fresh`
- `soybean_tofu_fresh`

The [Complete Content Index](/reference/content-index/) publishes the current registered IDs.

## Intermediates

Many foods are not final meals. Sauces, doughs, batters, seasonings, purees, boiled ingredients, fermented products, and other intermediates feed later recipes.

See [Sauces & Intermediates](/recipes/sauces-intermediates/) and [Complete Recipe Index](/recipes/complete-index/).

## Freshness

Perishable ingredients age through the [Freshness & Spoilage](/storage/freshness-spoilage/) system. Processing and cooking inherit freshness from perishable inputs rather than resetting food age to full freshness.
