## Environmental profiles

### Tomato

**Stages:** Germinating → Sprout → Leaf Growth → Budding → Flowering → Fruit Set → Fruit Growth → Ripe

- Soil saturation: 0.55-1.00, flood penalty 0.90
- Temperature: 8-38 °C survival range, 18-28 °C optimal
- Relative humidity: 30-95% survival range, 50-75% optimal
- Wind: comfortable through 7 m/s, damaging from 18 m/s
- Fertility consumption chance: 45%
- Harvest yield: 2-5
- Pollination: beneficial within 5 blocks, up to +25% growth boost
- Regrows after harvest from the Flowering stage
