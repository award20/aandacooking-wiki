Glass Jars are used for preservation, and they can now also be placed as blocks.

## Placeable jars

The placeable `aandacooking:glass_jar` and `aandacooking:sealed_glass_jar` have distinct appearances. Both are compact blocks that can be placed on a surface.

- Use a **Glass Jar Lid** on an open placed jar to change it into a Sealed Glass Jar. The lid is consumed in Survival
- **Sneak use** an empty hand on a placed Sealed Glass Jar to remove the seal, changing it back to the open jar and returning a Glass Jar Lid

## Crafting

A Sealed Glass Jar can also be crafted shapelessly using a Glass Jar and a Glass Jar Lid.

These placeable jar interactions are separate from the preservation data carried by filled jarred foods. For the process of sealing food, see the [Jar Sealer](/stations/jar-sealer/) and [Fermentation & Preservation](/recipes/fermentation-preservation/).
