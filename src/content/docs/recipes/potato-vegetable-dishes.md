---
title: Potato & Vegetable Dishes
description: Potato dishes, roasted and glazed vegetables, sauteed produce, and other vegetable focused foods in A & A Cooking.
---

Vegetables in A & A Cooking appear in several cooking styles instead of one generic recipe group. They can be roasted, sauteed, glazed, mashed, baked, added to soups and stews, or used as part of larger [Wok](/cooking/wok/) and [Skillet](/cooking/skillet/) dishes.

## Potato dishes

The potato branch includes several finished foods and preparation paths:

- `potato_mashed_garlic` (Garlic Mashed Potatoes)
- `potato_hash_herb` (Herb Potato Hash)
- `potato_baked_cheese_raw` (Raw Cheese Baked Potato)
- `potato_baked_cheese` (Cheese Baked Potato)
- `potato_soup` (Potato Soup)
- `potato_soup_creamy` (Creamy Potato Soup)

The soup variants are also listed on [Soups & Stews](/recipes/soups-stews/), while the cheese branch connects to [Eggs & Dairy](/recipes/eggs-dairy/).

## Carrot dishes

Carrots support both oven and stovetop dishes:

- `carrot_roasted` (Roasted Carrot)
- `carrot_glazed` (Glazed Carrots)
- `carrot_soup` (Carrot Soup)

Carrots also appear as ingredients in mixed vegetable recipes, soups, stir fries, and other multi ingredient dishes.

## Beetroot dishes

The Beetroot branch includes:

- `beetroot_roasted` (Roasted Beetroot)
- `beetroot_glazed` (Glazed Beetroot)
- `beetroot_soup_hearty` (Hearty Beetroot Soup)

Beetroot Juice is documented separately with [Drinks](/recipes/drinks/).

## Pumpkin dishes

Pumpkin is used for both savory and sweet recipes:

- `pumpkin_roasted` (Roasted Pumpkin)
- `pumpkin_puree` (Pumpkin Puree)
- `pumpkin_soup_creamy` (Creamy Pumpkin Soup)
- `pumpkin_bread_batter` (Pumpkin Bread Batter)
- `pumpkin_bread` (Pumpkin Bread)

This makes Pumpkin Puree a useful bridge between vegetable preparation and baking.

## Tomatoes and mushrooms

Smaller direct heat vegetable dishes include:

- `tomato_sauteed` (Sauteed Tomatoes)
- `mushroom_sauteed_garlic` (Sauteed Garlic Mushrooms)

Tomatoes also feed into salsa, sauces, pasta, soups, and bruschetta, while mushrooms connect to soups, sauces, pasta, stir fries, and Rice dishes.

## Mixed vegetable dishes

The broader vegetable catalog includes foods such as:

- `vegetable_stew` (Vegetable Stew)
- `vegetable_stir_fry` (Vegetable Stir Fry)
- `vegetable_stir_fry_seasoned` (Seasoned Vegetable Stir Fry)
- `egg_omelette_vegetable` (Vegetable Omelette)
- `rice_fried_vegetable` (Vegetable Fried Rice)
- `wheat_noodles_stir_fry_vegetable` (Vegetable Noodle Stir Fry)

These dishes are cross listed on their main cooking family pages so players can find them by either ingredient or cooking style.

## Cooking methods

Vegetable dishes can use several stations and cookware types depending on the recipe:

- [Stovetop/Oven](/stations/stovetop-oven/) for roasted and baked vegetables
- [Skillet](/cooking/skillet/) for sauteed and pan cooked foods
- [Wok](/cooking/wok/) for stir fries
- [Stockpot](/cooking/stockpot/) or [Saucepan](/cooking/saucepan/) for applicable soups, stews, purees, and reductions
- [Mixing Bowl](/stations/mixing-bowl/) or [Blender](/stations/blender/) for applicable intermediates

The individual recipe determines the required station, preparation state, temperature, time, and cookware.
