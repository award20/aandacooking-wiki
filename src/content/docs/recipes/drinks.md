A & A Cooking includes several beverage families that connect produce, herbs, dairy, and Soybean processing. This page records the established drink outputs while detailed quantities, processing times, and exact station requirements are verified recipe by recipe.

## Juices

The current drink catalog includes three established fruit and vegetable juices:

| ID | Display name | Family |
|---|---|---|
| `orange_juice` | Orange Juice | Fruit juice |
| `apple_juice` | Apple Juice | Fruit juice |
| `tomato_juice` | Tomato Juice | Vegetable juice |

Juices are useful examples of ingredients becoming both finished consumables and possible prerequisites for later recipes. Their exact current processing definitions will be added when the latest detailed recipe entries are checked.

## Smoothies

The established smoothie line includes:

- `orange_smoothie_creamy` (Creamy Orange Smoothie)
- `apple_smoothie_creamy` (Creamy Apple Smoothie)
- `pumpkin_smoothie_creamy` (Creamy Pumpkin Smoothie)
- `sweet_berry_smoothie` (Sweet Berry Smoothie)

These drinks connect the beverage catalog with the [Blender](/stations/blender/) and the dairy or produce chains used by their recipes. Exact ingredient counts and processing requirements still need verification against the latest recipe definitions.

## Herb teas

The herb system contributes several hot drinks:

| ID | Display name |
|---|---|
| `mint_tea` | Mint Tea |
| `rosemary_tea` | Rosemary Tea |
| `thyme_tea` | Thyme Tea |
| `mint_tea_sweet` | Sweet Mint Tea |

These recipes give herbs a use outside seasoning savory foods. The sweet Mint Tea branch also shows how a finished drink can build on an earlier beverage rather than beginning from raw ingredients every time.

See [Herbs & Seasonings](/recipes/herbs-seasonings/) for the herb ingredients behind these drinks.

## Hot chocolate

The dairy line includes:

- `milk_hot_chocolate` (Hot Chocolate)
- `milk_hot_chocolate_mint` (Mint Hot Chocolate)

Mint Hot Chocolate connects the dairy and herb systems. The drink is also relevant to the [Saucepan](/cooking/saucepan/) because hot liquid recipes need enough cookware capacity for their complete ingredient and liquid requirements.

## Soybean drinks

The Soybean production chain includes:

- `soybean_milk` (Soy Milk)
- `soybean_milk_sweet` (Sweet Soy Milk)

Soy Milk also functions as part of the broader Soybean processing tree rather than only as a final beverage. See [Rice & Soybean](/ingredients/rice-soybean/) for that production chain.

## Freshness and storage

Perishable beverages participate in the same freshness and storage systems used by other foods where supported by the current implementation. Refrigerated and frozen storage behavior is documented under [Cold Storage](/storage/cold-storage/).

## Recipe detail status

The drink outputs on this page are part of the established content catalog. Exact quantities, temperatures, liquid amounts, processing times, and station modes will be added as the latest individual recipe definitions are verified.
