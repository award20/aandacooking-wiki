Fresh Cheese is a dairy intermediate produced by gently heating [Cream](/recipes/details/cream/) in the [Saucepan](/cooking/saucepan/). It can be shredded on the [Cutting Board](/stations/cutting-board/) for later recipes such as [Cheese Sauce](/recipes/details/cheese-sauce/).

## Recipe

| Requirement | Amount |
|---|---:|
| [Cream](/recipes/details/cream/) | 3 |

**Station:** [Saucepan](/cooking/saucepan/)  
**Cooking time:** 18 seconds  
**Temperature:** 72 to 86 °C  
**Water:** None  
**Output:** 1 Fresh Cheese

Add the [Cream](/recipes/details/cream/) to a dry [Saucepan](/cooking/saucepan/) and warm it gently within the required temperature range until it thickens into Fresh Cheese.

## [Shredded Cheese](/recipes/details/shredded-cheese/)

Fresh Cheese has a registered shredding path on the [Cutting Board](/stations/cutting-board/). Shredding requires **4 preparation actions** and produces [Shredded Cheese](/recipes/details/shredded-cheese/) for recipes that require the shredded preparation state.
