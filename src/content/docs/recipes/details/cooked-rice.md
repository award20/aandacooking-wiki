Cooked Rice is a foundational starch used throughout fried Rice, bowls, porridge, and other Rice based dishes.

## Recipe

| Requirement | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Washed Rice | 2 |
| Water | 750 to 1250 mB |

**Station:** [Saucepan](/cooking/saucepan/)  
**Cooking time:** 14 seconds  
**Temperature:** 96 to 104 °C  
**Output:** 2 Cooked Rice

Wash the Rice first, then add it to the [Saucepan](/cooking/saucepan/) with enough water to remain within the recipe's liquid range. Cook until the Rice is tender and the recipe completes.
