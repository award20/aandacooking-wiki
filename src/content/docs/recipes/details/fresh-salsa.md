Fresh Salsa is a prepared condiment made from diced Tomato, diced Onion, and minced Garlic in the [Mixing Bowl](/stations/mixing-bowl/).

## Recipe

| Requirement | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Diced Tomato | 2 |
| Diced Onion | 1 |
| Minced Garlic | 1 |

**Station:** [Mixing Bowl](/stations/mixing-bowl/)  
**Mode:** Mix  
**Processing:** 8 Mix actions  
**Output:** 1 Fresh Salsa

## Preparation

Wash and dice the Tomatoes and Onion, then wash, peel, and mince the Garlic on the [Cutting Board](/stations/cutting-board/). Add all prepared ingredients to the [Mixing Bowl](/stations/mixing-bowl/) and mix until evenly combined.
