Seed Oil is a cooking intermediate produced from Wheat Seeds in the [Blender](/stations/blender/). It is used by recipes that require a prepared cooking oil, including [Mayonnaise](/recipes/details/mayonnaise/).

## Recipe

| Requirement | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Wheat Seeds | 3 |

**Station:** [Blender](/stations/blender/)  
**Mode:** Pulse  
**Processing:** 1 pulse cycle  
**Pulse cycle:** 40 ticks  
**Output:** 1 Seed Oil

Add the Wheat Seeds to the [Blender](/stations/blender/) and process them in Pulse mode until one cycle completes.

## Item and recipe ID

`aandacooking:seed_oil`
