Tomato Sauce is a [Saucepan](/cooking/saucepan/) intermediate used by several later dishes and sauce chains.

## Recipe

| Requirement | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Diced Tomatoes | 2 |
| Minced Onion | 1 |
| Minced Garlic | 1 |
| Water | 1 to 1.5 L |

**Station:** [Saucepan](/cooking/saucepan/)  
**Cooking time:** 12 seconds after reducing  
**Temperature:** 92 to 105 °C  
**Required reduction:** roughly 20 to 70 percent  
**Output:** 1 Tomato Sauce

## Preparation

Before cooking:

- wash and dice the Tomatoes
- wash and mince the Onion
- wash, peel, and mince the Garlic

Add the prepared ingredients and water to the [Saucepan](/cooking/saucepan/), bring the mixture into its cooking range, and allow it to reduce. The recipe completes while the liquid reduction remains within the required range.
