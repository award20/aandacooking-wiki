Mayonnaise is a condiment prepared by whisking [Beaten Egg](/recipes/details/beaten-egg/) with [Seed Oil](/recipes/details/seed-oil/) in the [Mixing Bowl](/stations/mixing-bowl/). One recipe execution produces **2 portions**.

## Recipe

| Requirement | Amount |
|---|---:|
| [Beaten Egg](/recipes/details/beaten-egg/) | 1 |
| [Seed Oil](/recipes/details/seed-oil/) | 1 |

**Station:** [Mixing Bowl](/stations/mixing-bowl/)  
**Mode:** Whisk  
**Processing:** 12 whisk actions  
**Output:** 2 Mayonnaise

Add the [Beaten Egg](/recipes/details/beaten-egg/) and [Seed Oil](/recipes/details/seed-oil/) to the [Mixing Bowl](/stations/mixing-bowl/) and whisk until the mixture becomes thick and emulsified.
