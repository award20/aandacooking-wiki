Tomato Paste is a concentrated tomato intermediate cooked in the [Saucepan](/cooking/saucepan/). It is a prerequisite for [Ketchup](/recipes/details/ketchup/) and other tomato based recipe chains.

## Recipe

| Requirement | Amount |
|---|---:|
| [Tomato Puree](/recipes/details/tomato-puree/) | 2 |

**Station:** [Saucepan](/cooking/saucepan/)  
**Cooking time:** 16 seconds  
**Temperature:** 88 to 102 °C  
**Water:** None  
**Output:** 1 Tomato Paste

## Preparation

Prepare [Tomato Puree](/recipes/details/tomato-puree/) in the [Blender](/stations/blender/), then add two portions to a dry [Saucepan](/cooking/saucepan/). Cook within the required temperature range until the puree becomes thick and concentrated.
