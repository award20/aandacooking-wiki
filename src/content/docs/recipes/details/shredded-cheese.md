Shredded Cheese is a prepared cheese ingredient made from [Fresh Cheese](/recipes/details/fresh-cheese/) on the [Cutting Board](/stations/cutting-board/). It is required by [Cheese Sauce](/recipes/details/cheese-sauce/) and several finished cheese dishes.

## Preparation

| Requirement | Amount |
|---|---:|
| [Fresh Cheese](/recipes/details/fresh-cheese/) | 1 |

**Station:** [Cutting Board](/stations/cutting-board/)  
**Action:** Shred  
**Processing:** 4 preparation actions  
**Output:** 1 Shredded Cheese

Place [Fresh Cheese](/recipes/details/fresh-cheese/) on the [Cutting Board](/stations/cutting-board/), select the Shred preparation, and complete four actions.
