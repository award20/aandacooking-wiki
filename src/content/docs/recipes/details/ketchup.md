Ketchup is a tomato condiment cooked from [Tomato Paste](/recipes/details/tomato-paste/), Sugar, and [Onion Powder](/recipes/details/onion-powder/) in the [Saucepan](/cooking/saucepan/).

## Recipe

| Requirement | Amount |
|---|---:|
| [Tomato Paste](/recipes/details/tomato-paste/) | 1 |
| Sugar | 1 |
| [Onion Powder](/recipes/details/onion-powder/) | 1 |

**Station:** [Saucepan](/cooking/saucepan/)  
**Cooking time:** 12 seconds  
**Temperature:** 82 to 96 °C  
**Water:** None  
**Output:** 1 Ketchup

## Preparation

Prepare [Tomato Paste](/recipes/details/tomato-paste/) in the [Saucepan](/cooking/saucepan/) and [Onion Powder](/recipes/details/onion-powder/) in the [Mortar and Pestle](/stations/mortar-and-pestle/). Combine the ingredients in a dry saucepan and cook until smooth and glossy.
