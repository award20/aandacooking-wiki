Wheat Flour is a foundational baking intermediate prepared in the [Mortar and Pestle](/stations/mortar-and-pestle/). It feeds into dough, batter, noodles, and other baking chains.

## Recipe

| Requirement | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Wheat | 2 |

**Station:** [Mortar and Pestle](/stations/mortar-and-pestle/)  
**Mode:** Grind  
**Processing:** 10 grind actions  
**Output:** 2 Wheat Flour

Add the Wheat to the mortar and grind it until the processing requirement is complete.
