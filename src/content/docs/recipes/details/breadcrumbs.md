Breadcrumbs are a dry cooking intermediate prepared from Bread in the [Mortar and Pestle](/stations/mortar-and-pestle/). They can be used directly or combined with [Garlic Paste](/recipes/details/garlic-paste/) to make [Seasoned Breadcrumbs](/recipes/details/seasoned-breadcrumbs/).

## Recipe

| Requirement | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Bread | 1 |

**Station:** [Mortar and Pestle](/stations/mortar-and-pestle/)  
**Mode:** Crush  
**Processing:** 5 Crush actions  
**Output:** 2 Breadcrumbs

## Preparation

Add one Bread to the [Mortar and Pestle](/stations/mortar-and-pestle/) and crush it into coarse crumbs.
