Wheat Bread is baked from [Bread Dough](/recipes/details/bread-dough/) in an [iron baking dish](/cooking/oven-bakeware/).

## Recipe

| Requirement | Amount |
|---|---:|
| [Bread Dough](/recipes/details/bread-dough/) | 1 |

**Station:** [Stovetop/Oven](/stations/stovetop-oven/)  
**Cookware:** [Iron Baking Dish](/cooking/oven-bakeware/)  
**Cooking time:** 40 seconds  
**Temperature:** 180 to 200 °C  
**Output:** 1 Wheat Bread

Preheat the oven, place an [iron baking dish](/cooking/oven-bakeware/) on a rack, put the [Bread Dough](/recipes/details/bread-dough/) in the dish, and bake it within the required temperature range until the recipe completes.
