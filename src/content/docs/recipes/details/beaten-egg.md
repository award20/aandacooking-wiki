Beaten Egg is a foundational egg intermediate prepared in the [Mixing Bowl](/stations/mixing-bowl/). It is used by later recipes such as [Mayonnaise](/recipes/details/mayonnaise/), batters, custards, and other egg based foods.

## Recipe

| Requirement | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Cracked Eggs | 2 |

**Station:** [Mixing Bowl](/stations/mixing-bowl/)  
**Mode:** Whisk  
**Processing:** 12 whisk actions  
**Output:** 1 Beaten Egg

## Preparation

Crack two Eggs before placing them in the [Mixing Bowl](/stations/mixing-bowl/). Whisk the cracked Eggs until the processing requirement is complete.
