Herb Seasoning is a reusable savory blend prepared from processed Basil, Rosemary, and Thyme in the [Mortar and Pestle](/stations/mortar-and-pestle/).

## Recipe

| Requirement | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Crushed Basil | 1 |
| Ground Rosemary | 1 |
| Ground Thyme | 1 |

**Station:** [Mortar and Pestle](/stations/mortar-and-pestle/)  
**Mode:** Grind  
**Processing:** 10 Grind actions  
**Output:** 3 Herb Seasoning

## Preparation

Prepare the Basil, Rosemary, and Thyme in the [Mortar and Pestle](/stations/mortar-and-pestle/), combine the processed herbs, then grind them together until evenly blended.
