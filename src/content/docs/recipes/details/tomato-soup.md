Tomato Soup is a [Stockpot](/cooking/stockpot/) recipe built from washed and prepared vegetables with water. One recipe execution produces **4 servings**.

## Recipe

| Requirement | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Diced Tomatoes | 2 |
| Diced Onion | 1 |
| Minced Garlic | 1 |
| Water | 2 L |

**Station:** [Stockpot](/cooking/stockpot/)  
**Cooking time:** 20 seconds  
**Temperature:** 90 to 98 °C  
**Output:** 4 Tomato Soup

## Preparation

Before cooking:

- wash the Tomatoes, Onion, and Garlic
- dice the Tomatoes and Onion
- peel and mince the Garlic

The prepared ingredients and water are added to the [Stockpot](/cooking/stockpot/) and held at a gentle simmer until the recipe completes.
