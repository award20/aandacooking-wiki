Kelp Powder is a dry savory seasoning prepared from Dried Kelp in the [Mortar and Pestle](/stations/mortar-and-pestle/). It is one of the ingredients used to make [Savory Seasoning](/recipes/details/savory-seasoning/).

## Recipe

| Requirement | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Dried Kelp | 2 |

**Station:** [Mortar and Pestle](/stations/mortar-and-pestle/)  
**Mode:** Grind  
**Processing:** 10 Grind actions  
**Output:** 1 Kelp Powder

## Preparation

Add two Dried Kelp to the [Mortar and Pestle](/stations/mortar-and-pestle/) and grind until a fine savory powder forms.
