Cheese Sauce is a dairy sauce made from [Shredded Cheese](/recipes/details/shredded-cheese/), [Cream](/recipes/details/cream/), and [Butter](/recipes/details/butter/) in the [Saucepan](/cooking/saucepan/). The [Shredded Cheese](/recipes/details/shredded-cheese/) can be prepared from [Fresh Cheese](/recipes/details/fresh-cheese/) on the [Cutting Board](/stations/cutting-board/).

## Recipe

| Requirement | Amount |
|---|---:|
| [Shredded Cheese](/recipes/details/shredded-cheese/) | 1 |
| [Cream](/recipes/details/cream/) | 1 |
| [Butter](/recipes/details/butter/) | 1 |

**Station:** [Saucepan](/cooking/saucepan/)  
**Cooking time:** 10 seconds  
**Temperature:** 72 to 88 °C  
**Water:** None  
**Output:** 1 Cheese Sauce

## Preparation

Prepare [Fresh Cheese](/recipes/details/fresh-cheese/) and shred it on the [Cutting Board](/stations/cutting-board/). Combine the [Shredded Cheese](/recipes/details/shredded-cheese/), [Cream](/recipes/details/cream/), and [Butter](/recipes/details/butter/) in a dry [Saucepan](/cooking/saucepan/) and warm gently until smooth and evenly melted.
