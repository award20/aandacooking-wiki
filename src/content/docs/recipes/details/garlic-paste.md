Garlic Paste is a savory intermediate prepared from peeled Garlic in the [Mortar and Pestle](/stations/mortar-and-pestle/). It is used in recipes including [Seasoned Breadcrumbs](/recipes/details/seasoned-breadcrumbs/) and several cooked dishes.

## Recipe

| Requirement | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Peeled Garlic | 2 |

**Station:** [Mortar and Pestle](/stations/mortar-and-pestle/)  
**Mode:** Grind  
**Processing:** 10 Grind actions  
**Output:** 1 Garlic Paste

## Preparation

Wash and peel the Garlic, then add two peeled Garlic to the [Mortar and Pestle](/stations/mortar-and-pestle/) and grind until a smooth paste forms.
