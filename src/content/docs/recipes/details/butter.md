Butter is a dairy intermediate made by whisking [Cream](/recipes/details/cream/) in the [Mixing Bowl](/stations/mixing-bowl/). It is used across baking, sauces, direct cooking, and flavored butter recipes.

## Recipe

| Requirement | Amount |
|---|---:|
| [Cream](/recipes/details/cream/) | 2 |

**Station:** [Mixing Bowl](/stations/mixing-bowl/)  
**Mode:** Whisk  
**Processing:** 12 Whisk actions  
**Output:** 1 Butter

Add two [Cream](/recipes/details/cream/) to the [Mixing Bowl](/stations/mixing-bowl/) and whisk until the dairy separates and Butter forms.
