Garlic Powder is a dry seasoning intermediate prepared from minced Garlic in the [Mortar and Pestle](/stations/mortar-and-pestle/). It is one of the three powders used to make [Savory Seasoning](/recipes/details/savory-seasoning/).

## Recipe

| Requirement | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Minced Garlic | 2 |

**Station:** [Mortar and Pestle](/stations/mortar-and-pestle/)  
**Mode:** Grind  
**Processing:** 10 Grind actions  
**Output:** 1 Garlic Powder

## Preparation

Wash and peel the Garlic, mince it on the [Cutting Board](/stations/cutting-board/), then grind two portions in the [Mortar and Pestle](/stations/mortar-and-pestle/) until a fine powder forms.
