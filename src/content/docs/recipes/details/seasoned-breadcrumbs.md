Seasoned Breadcrumbs combine [Breadcrumbs](/recipes/details/breadcrumbs/) with [Garlic Paste](/recipes/details/garlic-paste/) in the [Mixing Bowl](/stations/mixing-bowl/). They are used by breaded meat and fish recipes.

## Recipe

| Requirement | Amount |
|---|---:|
| [Breadcrumbs](/recipes/details/breadcrumbs/) | 2 |
| [Garlic Paste](/recipes/details/garlic-paste/) | 1 |

**Station:** [Mixing Bowl](/stations/mixing-bowl/)  
**Mode:** Mix  
**Processing:** 8 Mix actions  
**Output:** 2 Seasoned Breadcrumbs

## Preparation

Prepare the [Breadcrumbs](/recipes/details/breadcrumbs/) and [Garlic Paste](/recipes/details/garlic-paste/), add them to the [Mixing Bowl](/stations/mixing-bowl/), then mix until the [Garlic Paste](/recipes/details/garlic-paste/) is evenly distributed.
