Powdered Sugar is a baking intermediate made by grinding Sugar in the [Mortar and Pestle](/stations/mortar-and-pestle/).

## Recipe

| Requirement | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Sugar | 2 |

**Station:** [Mortar and Pestle](/stations/mortar-and-pestle/)  
**Mode:** Grind  
**Processing:** 10 Grind actions  
**Output:** 2 Powdered Sugar

## Preparation

Add two Sugar to the [Mortar and Pestle](/stations/mortar-and-pestle/) and grind until very fine.
