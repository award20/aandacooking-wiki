Fresh Tofu is a Soybean intermediate made by gently heating Soy Milk with Apple Vinegar in a dry [Saucepan](/cooking/saucepan/).

## Recipe

| Requirement | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Soy Milk | 2 |
| Apple Vinegar | 1 |

**Station:** [Saucepan](/cooking/saucepan/)  
**Cooking time:** 12 seconds  
**Temperature:** 78 to 90 °C  
**Water:** none  
**Output:** 2 Fresh Tofu

Prepare Soy Milk and Apple Vinegar first. Add both ingredients to a dry [Saucepan](/cooking/saucepan/) and warm them gently until the Soy Milk coagulates into Fresh Tofu.
