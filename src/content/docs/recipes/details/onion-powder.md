Onion Powder is a dry seasoning intermediate prepared from diced Onion in the [Mortar and Pestle](/stations/mortar-and-pestle/). It is used by recipes such as [Ketchup](/recipes/details/ketchup/) and [Savory Seasoning](/recipes/details/savory-seasoning/).

## Recipe

| Requirement | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Diced Onion | 2 |

**Station:** [Mortar and Pestle](/stations/mortar-and-pestle/)  
**Mode:** Grind  
**Processing:** 10 Grind actions  
**Output:** 1 Onion Powder

## Preparation

Wash and dice the Onion on the [Cutting Board](/stations/cutting-board/), then grind two portions in the [Mortar and Pestle](/stations/mortar-and-pestle/) until a fine powder forms.
