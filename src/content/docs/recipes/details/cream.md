Cream is a dairy intermediate used by cheese, butter, baking, soup, sauce, drink, and dessert recipes.

## Recipe

| Requirement | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Milk Bucket | 1 |

**Station:** Crafting grid  
**Recipe:** Shapeless  
**Processing:** Instant  
**Output:** 2 Cream  
**Container:** The empty Bucket is returned

Place one Milk Bucket in any crafting grid to produce two Cream.

## Item and recipe ID

`aandacooking:milk_cream`
