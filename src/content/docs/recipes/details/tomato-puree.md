Tomato Puree is a smooth tomato intermediate prepared in the [Blender](/stations/blender/). It is used by concentrated tomato recipes such as [Tomato Paste](/recipes/details/tomato-paste/).

## Recipe

| Requirement | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Diced Tomatoes | 2 |

**Station:** [Blender](/stations/blender/)  
**Mode:** Blend  
**Processing:** 1 Blend cycle, 100 game ticks  
**Output:** 1 Tomato Puree

## Preparation

Wash and dice two Tomatoes, add them to the [Blender](/stations/blender/), and run one Blend cycle until smooth.
