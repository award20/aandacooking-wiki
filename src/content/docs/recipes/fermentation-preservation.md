Fermentation is an implemented production system in A & A Cooking. It is used for ingredients that need time-based processing rather than immediate mixing or direct heat.

## Fermentation Crock

The [Fermentation Crock](/stations/fermentation-crock/) is the dedicated station for fermentation recipes.

The retained implementation record confirms that fermentation progress:

- persists through save/load
- pauses when the output cannot be accepted
- integrates with Load Pinned
- participates in Recipe Book prerequisite planning
- can access the connected kitchen storage network

A fermentation execution still requires its full processing time. Bulk or queued recipe behavior does not bypass fermentation time.

## Implemented fermentation chain examples

The current content line includes:

- Apple Vinegar
- Fermented Soybeans
- Fresh Tofu
- Salt Brine
- Soy Sauce Mash
- Soy Sauce
- brine-fermented vegetables
- pickled ingredients used by later recipes

Exact ingredient quantities and fermentation durations will be added to individual recipe pages when the complete current recipe registry is imported into the wiki.

## Salt and brine

Salt is a survival pantry ingredient obtained through structure loot in the retained content snapshot. It is **not** generated by drawing water from the Kitchen Sink.

Salt Brine is a recipe intermediate used by preservation and later food-production recipes. Because it is a real recipe input, compatible stations must accept it even when it is not represented by a normal raw-ingredient profile.

## Fermentation as a dependency

Fermented products can become prerequisites for later dishes. This makes fermentation part of the Recipe Book dependency graph rather than a completely separate crafting system.

For example, a recipe may require a preserved vegetable, tofu, Soy Sauce, or another fermented intermediate before the final Wok, Skillet, Stockpot, or Mixing Bowl step can begin.

## Preservation beyond fermentation

The mod already has freshness, refrigeration, freezing, and fermentation systems. Broader preservation methods such as additional canning, drying, or smoking systems remain separate roadmap work unless documented as implemented on a future page.
