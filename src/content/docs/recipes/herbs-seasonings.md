Herbs in A & A Cooking are used as ingredients, preparation targets, seasonings, drinks, and prerequisites for larger dishes. The established herb set includes Basil, Rosemary, Thyme, Parsley, and Mint.

## Fresh herbs

The current herb family includes:

- Basil
- Rosemary
- Thyme
- Parsley
- Mint

These ingredients appear across savory foods, breads, pasta, drinks, and seasoning recipes. Their full crop profiles are listed on [Current Crops](/agriculture/crops/).

## Seeds

The established herb content also includes seed items for the herb crops:

- Basil Seeds
- Rosemary Seeds
- Thyme Seeds
- Parsley Seeds
- Mint Seeds

Planting, season preference, climate ranges, yield, vigor, pollination, and regrowth values are documented on [Current Crops](/agriculture/crops/).

## Processed herb forms

Several herbs have prepared forms used by later recipes:

| ID | Display name | Preparation family |
|---|---|---|
| `basil_crushed` | Crushed Basil | Crushed herb |
| `rosemary_ground` | Ground Rosemary | Ground herb |
| `thyme_ground` | Ground Thyme | Ground herb |
| `mint_crushed` | Crushed Mint | Crushed herb |
| `herb_seasoning` | [Herb Seasoning](/recipes/details/herb-seasoning/) | Seasoning blend |

The [Mortar and Pestle](/stations/mortar-and-pestle/) handles Crush and Grind processing. Exact herb recipe inputs and actions are listed in the [Complete Recipe Index](/recipes/complete-index/).

Herbs also support a dedicated tea line:

See [Drinks](/recipes/drinks/) for the beverage catalog.

## Herb foods

Established herb based dishes and intermediates include:

- `tomato_sauce_herb` (Herb Tomato Sauce)
- `chicken_seared_herb` (Herb Seared Chicken)
- `potato_hash_herb` (Herb Potato Hash)
- `tomato_pasta_herb` (Herb Tomato Pasta)
- `mushroom_pasta_herb` (Herb Mushroom Pasta)
- `chicken_pasta_creamy_herb` (Creamy Herb Chicken Pasta)
- `tomato_bruschetta` (Tomato Bruschetta)
- `bread_garlic_herb` (Herb Garlic Bread)
- `tomato_pasta_basil` (Basil Tomato Pasta)
- `vegetable_soup_herb` (Herb Vegetable Soup)
- `wheat_focaccia_dough` (Focaccia Dough)
- `wheat_focaccia_herb` (Herb Focaccia)

This list shows how herbs feed multiple recipe families rather than forming an isolated branch. Some dishes use fresh herbs directly, while others use crushed, ground, or blended seasoning forms.

## Seasoning role

[Herb Seasoning](/recipes/details/herb-seasoning/) acts as a reusable intermediate for recipes that call for a prepared herb blend. It belongs with the broader [Sauces & Intermediates](/recipes/sauces-intermediates/) system because the blend can sit between raw herbs and the finished dish.
