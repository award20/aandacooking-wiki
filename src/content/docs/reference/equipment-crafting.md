A & A Cooking alpha.2 includes crafting recipes for its kitchen equipment and utility content. Shaped recipes still require their normal crafting grid arrangement; the tables below summarize the required materials and output.

## Wood kitchen variants

Kitchen Counters, Kitchen Cabinets, Kitchen Sinks, and Cutting Boards are available in Oak, Spruce, Birch, Jungle, Acacia, Dark Oak, Mangrove, Cherry, Pale Oak, Bamboo, Crimson, and Warped materials.

| Output family | Recipe ID pattern | Type | Materials | Output count |
|---|---|---|---|---:|
| [Kitchen Counter](/storage/cabinets-counters/) | `aandacooking:<material>_kitchen_counter` | Shaped | 3 matching Slabs, 5 matching Planks | 1 |
| [Kitchen Cabinet](/storage/cabinets-counters/) | `aandacooking:<material>_kitchen_cabinet` | Shaped | 3 matching Slabs, 5 matching Planks, Chest | 1 |
| [Kitchen Sink](/stations/kitchen-sink/) | `aandacooking:<material>_kitchen_sink` | Shaped | 3 matching Slabs, 5 matching Planks, Cauldron | 1 |
| [Cutting Board](/stations/cutting-board/) | `aandacooking:<material>_cutting_board` | Shaped | 3 matching Slabs | 1 |

See [Wood Kitchen Variants](/reference/wood-kitchen-variants/) for every exact block and recipe ID.

## Kitchen knives

Both Kitchen Knives use the same diagonal blade and handle layout for their direct shaped recipes.

| Output | Recipe ID | Type | Materials | Output count |
|---|---|---|---|---:|
| Iron Kitchen Knife | `aandacooking:iron_kitchen_knife` | Shaped | Iron Ingot, Stick | 1 |
| Stainless Steel Kitchen Knife | `aandacooking:stainless_steel_kitchen_knife` | Shaped | Stainless Steel Ingot, Stick | 1 |
| Stainless Steel Kitchen Knife | `aandacooking:stainless_steel_kitchen_knife_upgrade` | Shapeless | Iron Kitchen Knife, Stainless Steel Ingot | 1 |

The Stainless Steel Kitchen Knife can therefore be crafted directly or upgraded from the Iron Kitchen Knife.

## Cookware progression

The four heated cookware families follow the same material progression even though Stainless Steel and Diamond Coated cookware also have direct shaped recipes:

1. Iron
2. Stainless Steel
3. Diamond Coated
4. Netherite

See [Cookware Tiers](/cooking/cookware-tiers/) for the thermal differences between those materials.
