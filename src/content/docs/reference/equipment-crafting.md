## Cookware upgrade path

The four heated cookware families use the same material progression:

1. Craft the Iron vessel.
2. Combine it with one Stainless Steel Ingot.
3. Combine the Stainless Steel vessel with one Diamond.
4. Combine the Diamond Coated vessel with one Netherite Ingot.

See [Cookware Tiers](/cooking/cookware-tiers/) for the thermal differences between those materials.

## Stainless Steel

One **Stainless Steel Ingot** is crafted shapelessly from one Iron Ingot and one Copper Ingot. It is used for stainless steel cookware upgrades and the Stainless Steel Kitchen Knife.
