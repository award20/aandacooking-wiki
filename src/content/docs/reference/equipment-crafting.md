## Stainless Steel production

1. Combine **3 Iron Ingots**, **1 Copper Ingot**, and **1 Coal or Charcoal** shapelessly to obtain **4 Stainless Steel Blend**.
2. Process each Stainless Steel Blend in a **Blast Furnace** to receive a Stainless Steel Ingot. The blasting recipe takes **100 game ticks** per operation.

## Cooking equipment

The mod includes recipes for [Stockpots](/cooking/stockpot/), [Skillets](/cooking/skillet/), [Saucepans](/cooking/saucepan/), [Woks](/cooking/wok/), and [Oven Bakeware](/cooking/oven-bakeware/). Stainless Steel and Diamond Coated cookware can be crafted directly or upgraded by the available alternative recipes, and Netherite cookware uses the appropriate upgrade process.

The [Stovetop/Oven](/stations/stovetop-oven/) has both Cobblestone and Cobbled Deepslate crafting variants.
